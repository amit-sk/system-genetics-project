Section 1: Define research objective
====================================

Question 1 - Phenotypes
-----------------------

Phenotype 1 - ID 881: Striatum volume, dorsal striatum, bilateral and
adjusted for shrinkage, age, sex, plane of section, and BXD group/epoch
[mm^3]
(Rosen et al., Genes Brain Behav 2009; PMID 19191878)

Measurement: volume of the dorsal striatum (caudate-putamen, excluding
nucleus accumbens) estimated by Cavalieri point counting on every 10th
celloidin section (~30 um), blind to strain and sex. Volumes were corrected
for histological shrinkage using the brain volume at fixation. Each
subject's volume was then regressed against age, sex, plane of section,
BXD epoch (Taylor vs. UTHSC strains) and non-striatal brain weight, and the
trait is the mean residual per strain.

Experimental settings: 342 young-adult mice (50-100 days; 176 F, 166 M) from
53 BXD strains and the parental strains, obtained from the Mouse Brain
Library. Brains were perfusion-fixed, weighed, celloidin-embedded and cut in
the coronal or horizontal plane. The trait is the strain mean.


Phenotype 2 - ID 1231: Morphine response (50 mg/kg i.p.), locomotion from
0-180 min (total activity over the 3 hour test) after injection in an
activity chamber, females [cm]
(Philip et al., Genes Brain Behav 2010; PMID 19958391)

Measurement: total horizontal distance travelled (cm) during the 3 h after a
single i.p. injection of morphine sulfate (50 mg/kg), recorded as photocell
beam breaks in an automated open-field chamber (Med Associates ENV-515,
43 x 43 cm) and logged in 15 min bins. Trait 1231 is the sum over all bins
for females (the individual bins are traits 1219, 1222, 1223, ...).

Experimental settings: part of the Tennessee Mouse Genome Consortium
high-throughput phenotyping of ~70 BXD strains (University of Memphis
morphine battery). Females tested at 8-9 weeks of age, ~6 mice per strain,
after >= 1 week acclimation; 14:10 light/dark cycle. Each mouse was run
through the battery in a fixed order (open field after saline, then open
field after morphine).

The two phenotypes are from different collections (neuroanatomy vs.
drug-response behaviour).


Question 2 - Gene expression datasets
-------------------------------------
Chosen: Hypothalamus (GSE36674) and Liver (GSE17522).

Hypothalamus: the only brain tissue available, and both phenotypes are
brain traits. Striatal volume is a CNS morphology trait; cis-regulatory
effects on gene expression are largely shared across brain regions, so
hypothalamic expression serves as a proxy for brain-wide effects of a
locus. Morphine-induced locomotion is centrally mediated through mu-opioid
receptors, which are densely expressed in the hypothalamus.

Liver: morphine is cleared mainly by hepatic glucuronidation (UGT enzymes),
so liver expression differences alter the effective drug exposure and
thereby the locomotor response. For striatal volume, Rosen et al. report
that the strongest correlate of the adjusted volume is transferrin
saturation (r = 0.66); transferrin is synthesized in the liver, making it a
plausible intermediate tissue for this trait as well.

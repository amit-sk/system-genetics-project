Discussion - problems / caveats in the experimental settings
-------------------------------------------------------------
- Sex composition of the expression data. Expression was averaged per
  strain regardless of sex. Most strains have one male and one female
  array, but 11 hypothalamus strains (6 M-only, 5 F-only) and 2 liver
  strains (BXD11 M-only, BXD33 F+F) are single-sex. For sex-biased genes
  (numerous in both hypothalamus and liver) the means of these strains
  are shifted relative to the mixed-sex strains, which can create spurious
  eQTLs or mask real ones. In addition, phenotype 1231 is females-only,
  whereas the expression means are mixed-sex, so the mediation test pairs
  a female trait with sex-averaged expression. Centring expression within
  sex before averaging (or using female arrays only for 1231) would have
  removed the additive sex effect; it was not done because it is not part
  of the required preprocessing.
